# Portfolio Summary

## Product Case: InstaHelp Real-Time Service Recovery

This project focuses on a high-stakes on-demand services problem: a customer books a home service, but the assigned professional cancels unexpectedly. The platform fails not because demand is low, but because the recovery flow is broken.

The solution is a recovery engine that can detect cancellations, match alternative professionals in real time, and give customers a clear path to either continue with a reassigned professional or receive a refund and support fallback. The system is designed to preserve trust, reduce churn, and protect contribution margin.

### Why it matters
- reliability is the true differentiator in premium home services
- cancellations create a trust gap and a support break
- customers should not be forced into a dead-end chatbot loop
- business value comes from reducing failed bookings and preserving premium pricing

### What the project demonstrates
- product strategy grounded in real business pain
- design thinking for critical service failure moments
- AI-assisted recovery and operational automation
- clear metrics thinking for reliability and retention
- portfolio-worthy storytelling with a real customer problem

### Project narrative
The project positions the product as a trust-preserving recovery system rather than a generic booking app. It shows how a platform must recover from failures quickly and respectfully without wasting the customer’s time or harming brand credibility.
